## How the parts fit

- **Header.** The `header` slot of [`kai-conversations`](/components/conversations/) replaces the built-in title bar. The search and collapse buttons are plain [`kai-button`](/components/button/)s, and the quick actions are interactive `kai-row`s. They only report a click. What a click does is the script's decision.
- **Folders.** A folder is a native `<details>`. The rail walks the rows inside open folders and skips the ones inside closed folders, and a summary keeps its own Enter and Space toggle. There is one tab stop into the rows, and arrows move across folders.
- **Search.** The button opens a [command palette](/patterns/command-trigger/) in a dialog. The chat list, the filter and what selecting a result does all live in your script, so the palette shows whatever you assign to `items`.
- **Collapse.** The button calls `collapseAside('start')` on the workspace. Swap it for whatever your layout does.
- **Active row.** The rail reports `kai-conversation-select`. Your script sets `activeId` in response, which is why the highlight follows your state and not the element's.

## Make it yours

Rows can be any `kai-conversation-item`, wrapped in whatever structure you want, and each takes a `menu` slot for your own row menu. Replace the hard-coded rows with a loop over your records. The pattern keeps ARIA valid by putting each folder's rows in a list inside a list item, so keep that shape if you change the markup.
